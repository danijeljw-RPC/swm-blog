## Your Spiritual / Personal Path

**How would you describe your path so far?**  
_Write this however you like. Spiritual, accidental, chaotic, sceptical, intuitive, lifelong, recent awakening — whatever is true._

**Was there a moment, experience, person, dream, loss, coincidence, or event that changed how you see reality?**
For me it's always been something I have been aware of that other people could not explain. I didn't see ghosts or dead people as a kid, but I noticed that I was different to most people because my ideas were always rooted in memories of the future. My mum always told me when I was smaller that I had _special_ abilities that not everyone else has, and my mum's grandma had abilities like that. Being able to hear things others couldn't when you lease expect it, or have an OVERWHELMING intuition into the most mundane things that 100% has never failed me - but I can't turn it on and off like a light switch. It happens when it happens. Since it's always been there since I can remember, it's never really been a point that changed my perception - I just grew into it as I got wiser with each year and the experiences kept piling on.

**What do you believe now that you never expected yourself to believe?**
That a time would come where I am writing a blog and doing a video podcast series for the world to see, and discuss my personal experiences, the experiences of other people - out in the open without having to hide away my secrets.

**What are you still completely unsure about?**
I know the connection to the afterlife has always demonstrated that our consciousness passes back to Home, but is it a copy of the consciousness that we have here and we won't remember it as ourselves, but as the original copy of ourselves? I wonder if a clone knows it's a clone, if it was never told it was a clone. Are we a clone of our Higher Selves that fragments and then sends a fax back Home when we are done here? I hope not!

**What does spirituality mean to you personally?**


**Do you consider yourself psychic, intuitive, sensitive, a light worker, star seed, seeker, sceptic, something else, or none of the above?**
I consider myself intuitive above all else. Intuition has guided me the strongest above all other skills. It has taken me some time to recognise that I also have a much deeper understanding of concepts and paradigms as soon as they are introduced to me. Almost as if I have a complete download from previous lives and the reason I am here - but I can't access it all at once unless I need that fragment of information then and there. I am also a star seed with the goal of taking on the role of a light worker. My goal is to bring the next level of knowledge to all those that are seeking a higher truth and to raise the vibration globally so we can transition to the next stage as a whole of humanity.

**How do you experience intuition or inner knowing?**
Very strongly when it's impractical, or a sense of awareness to an outcome from the current trajectory.

To help you the reader understand by what I mean strongly when it's impractical it's easier to refernce a simple example. I could be getting ready for work in the morning and then suddenly have an overwhelimg urge to get a black pen and bring it with me. What's wrong with that you ask? I only ever write in blue. I never write in black. Later in the day my boss might hand me a piece of paper to fill in and the instructions on the top of the page will clearly dictate "Must be completed with black biro only". Guess what? I brought my black pen to work today, which never gets used, and it's come in handy. Never in a million years would I know to bring or thought to bring a black pen. It's not something I could've planned because that form I needed to fill in came randomly from another department and needed to be filled in and sent back the same day. This can apply to any matter of things - scissors, eraser, notebook, sticky tape, $5 note, a $2 coin - anything. It's hard to describe, because it's not even like it's a memory or thought - it's an involuntary compulson. And can I ignore it? Sure I can - I will 100% regret it later that I didn't follow my intuition.

The other type of intuition, it's not like a gut feeling when you think a situation is going to be good or bad - like betting on a horse or this is a risky path to walk because you're going to walk into a spider web - this is almost like borrowing memories from my future self. Having almost a complete layout of the outcome of not just a situation - but an entire trajectory. It doesn't mean I know the finer details like someone is going to be wearing a blue jumper tomorrow. It's more like I just know the outcome of a series of events and how they play out. Imagine watching a TV show a few years ago, but not paying attention to it and doing housework so it's on in the background. You have the gist of it, but not the full scope of what happened in the shows. Then someone has it on TV years later and you know what's going to happen but don't remember you watched this show in the background years ago. It's like that.

Intuition can also be considered a form of pattern recognition - but you have no point of reference where the pattern is coming from. It's almost as if it should be apparent to everyone else yet you're the only one that seems to know what the storyline is progressing towards.

## Your Specialties / Interests

**What subjects do you naturally keep coming back to?**  
Ancient mysteries, aliens, higher self, Source, dreams, energy, dimensions and syncronicity. 

**What would you call your main speciality or strongest area of interest?**
Aliens, dreams and energy

**What topic could you talk about for hours without getting bored?**
Dreams. I haven't mentioned it much in this article about myself so far - but dreams. They are a fascinating subject for me and carry the most weight especially since the last dream I had with my mother. I was with her when she was in hospital for her last few days and stayed by her side day and night. The night she passed away I had a dream where her and I were talking, and then I woke up and she had passed away a few mins before. Though I do not remember exactly what we were talking about - I know it as comforting for me and peaceful for her. This is where our consciousness is able to travel back to the astral plane where the souls of life that has departed has returned Home and we can meet safely without crossing too far to the other side. It's also an anchored place we are able to explore our inner psychology and grow as a person. Dreams carry more than we allow ourselves to believe sometimes. I have nightmares too, but they no longer bring me stress. What would be considered a crazy nightmare keeping people from falling asleep has become an enjoyable dream for me because it allows me to reflect and explore other areas of my inner self without being harmed. My soul travels often and safely to and fro this world.

**What topic are you currently trying to understand better?**
I am trying to understand better what psychics are able to experience. I find it fascinating they can hear, see, feel, touch, speak to the spiritual energy of life forms - what is that really like? Do I want to explore that myself? How does it work? Fascinating!

**What subject do you disagree with people about most often?**
That bad people go to Hell. We don't. It's a hard pill to swallow, but Hitler is not in a firepit dancing on hot coals as we speak. Even though he committed atrocities, that was a soul contract, and he is back home in heaven. I'm happy to discuss it with anyone should they need to understand - but bad thing need to happen for spiritual growth to happen at a more rapid pace.

**What is one thing you think people dismiss too quickly?**
Crytals are conduits of energy if you lean into them and let them be an extension of your own energy. I have mini yellow quartz obelisk next to my bed because that helps me explore more dreams each time I sleep and remember them when I wake up. I find people dismiss that a piece of stone is able to channel that energy, but it really does. Oh, and aliens.
