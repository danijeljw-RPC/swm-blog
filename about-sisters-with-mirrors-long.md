# About Sisters with Mirrors

**Sisters with Mirrors** is a weekly exploration of consciousness, spirituality, mystery, the unseen, and the questions that sit just beyond the edge of what we think we know.

This is not a blog built around one belief system, one practice, or one answer.

It is a place to explore.

A place to question.

A place to look into the mirror — and then look beyond it.

Each week, we take on ideas that challenge the ordinary view of reality: consciousness, the higher self, Source, reincarnation, the afterlife, psychic experience, extraterrestrial intelligence, astrology, tarot, energy, synchronicity, spiritual awakening, multidimensional reality, and the strange connections that seem to appear when we begin paying closer attention.

Some conversations are deeply spiritual. Some are philosophical. Some are strange. Some are personal. Some begin with a simple question and end somewhere completely unexpected.

That is the point.

We are not here to tell you what to believe.

We are here to ask better questions.

---

## Step Through the Mirror

The mirror is more than a symbol for us.

It represents reflection, perception, identity, possibility, and the boundary between the reality we experience every day and the things that may exist beyond it.

What happens when we question the version of reality we have been handed?

What happens when intuition tells us something before logic catches up?

What happens when people describe near-death experiences, contact with the dead, past-life memories, prophetic dreams, synchronicities, unexplained knowing, or encounters they cannot easily explain?

What if consciousness is not produced entirely by the brain?

What if death is a transition rather than an ending?

What if the universe is not empty?

What if our lives are connected to something larger than ourselves?

And what if some of the things humanity once dismissed as impossible turn out to be parts of reality we simply do not yet understand?

Sisters with Mirrors exists in that space between certainty and possibility.

## Consciousness

Consciousness is one of the central threads running through everything we do.

We explore what consciousness is, where it may come from, whether it exists independently of the physical body, and whether it could extend beyond the limits of ordinary human perception.

That opens the door to questions around:

- higher consciousness
- altered states of awareness
- intuition and inner knowing
- consciousness beyond death
- collective consciousness
- universal consciousness
- multidimensional consciousness
- consciousness in animals and other life
- non-human consciousness
- the relationship between consciousness and reality itself

Science has made extraordinary progress in understanding the brain, but the subjective experience of being aware remains one of humanity's deepest mysteries.

We are interested in that mystery.

---

## The Higher Self

Many spiritual traditions describe a deeper or expanded aspect of the self — something beyond the everyday personality, fears, habits, and physical identity.

We explore the idea of the **higher self** as a greater level of awareness: the part of us that may see beyond the immediate moment, understand the lessons within our experiences, and remain connected to a larger consciousness.

Is intuition sometimes the higher self speaking?

Are moments of sudden clarity simply subconscious processing, or something more?

Can we become more aligned with that deeper part of ourselves?

And if a higher self exists, how does it relate to free will, destiny, reincarnation, and the lives we choose to live?

---

## Source, God and Universal Consciousness

Different traditions use different words: **Source**, God, the Universe, the Divine, universal consciousness, the creator, the field, the all.

We do not insist on one definition.

Instead, we explore the possibility that consciousness and existence may originate from, or remain connected to, something larger.

A recurring idea within Sisters with Mirrors is that individual consciousness may be less like an isolated flame and more like a branch, thread, spark, or expression of a greater whole.

If we all come from the same Source, what does that mean for identity?

For death?

For reincarnation?

For empathy?

For our connection to animals, nature, other people — and perhaps even intelligent life elsewhere in the universe?

These are enormous questions. We intend to ask them anyway.

## Reincarnation and Past Lives

The idea that consciousness may live more than once appears across cultures, religions, spiritual traditions, personal testimony, and modern investigations into unusual childhood memories.

We explore reincarnation without pretending every story proves it.

Topics may include:

- past-life memories
- recurring relationships across lifetimes
- soul groups
- karmic patterns
- lessons carried between lives
- unexplained fears or affinities
- birth and death as parts of a larger cycle
- whether we choose aspects of our lives before incarnation
- what happens between lives

If we have lived before, the implications are profound.

If we have not, the persistence of these experiences and beliefs is still worth understanding.

## Death, the Afterlife and What Comes Next

Death is one of the few experiences that touches every human life, yet it remains one of the subjects people are often least comfortable discussing.

We want to discuss it.

Not with fear, but with curiosity.

We explore ideas and experiences surrounding:

- the afterlife
- near-death experiences
- consciousness after bodily death
- spirits and apparitions
- communication with the deceased
- mediumship
- deathbed visions
- signs from loved ones
- the possibility of continuing awareness
- what different spiritual traditions believe happens after death

We do not claim to have the final answer.

We are interested in the evidence, the stories, the experiences, and the possibility that death may not be the end of consciousness.

## Psychic and Mediumistic Experience

Human beings have reported unusual forms of perception for thousands of years.

Knowing something before being told.

Dreaming about an event before it happens.

Feeling someone enter a room before seeing them.

Receiving a name, image, voice, impression, or message with no obvious source.

Sensing the presence of someone who has died.

Some experiences may have ordinary explanations. Others are harder to dismiss.

We explore psychic development, mediumship, clairvoyance, clairaudience, intuition, precognition, remote perception, energetic sensitivity, and the difference between genuine insight and the very human tendency to find patterns where none exist.

The goal is exploration without abandoning discernment.

The universe is vast.

The idea that Earth contains the only intelligent life in existence is a remarkable claim in itself.

Sisters with Mirrors explores extraterrestrial life not only as a scientific possibility, but also through the stranger questions surrounding consciousness and contact.

We discuss:

- extraterrestrial intelligence
- non-human intelligence (NHI)
- UFOs and UAP
- contact experiences
- alleged encounters
- consciousness-based contact
- whether advanced intelligence could interact with reality differently from us
- extraterrestrial spirituality
- the possibility of multidimensional beings
- humanity's place in a populated universe

We are particularly interested in the overlap between contact experiences, altered consciousness, spirituality, dreams, synchronicity, and reports of telepathic communication.

Sometimes the UFO question becomes a consciousness question very quickly.

## Dimensions, Timelines and the Nature of Reality

What if reality is stranger than the three dimensions of space and one dimension of time we experience every day?

Modern physics already describes a universe far less intuitive than ordinary life suggests.

Spiritual and metaphysical traditions go further, proposing multiple dimensions, parallel timelines, alternative realities, different planes of existence, and states of consciousness that may interact with them.

We explore these ideas as questions rather than settled facts.

What would a higher-dimensional perspective look like?

Could multiple possibilities exist simultaneously?

What is a timeline?

Can consciousness experience reality differently outside the physical body?

Where does science end, where does metaphysics begin, and where might the two eventually meet?

We explore tarot alongside other forms of divination, symbolism, sigils, synchronicity, dreams, numerology, and intuitive practices.

Dreams create entire realities while we sleep.

People appear as themselves — or as symbolic stand-ins for someone else.

Places change.

Time behaves strangely.

The dead return.

Impossible events feel completely normal.

And occasionally, a dream feels different from an ordinary dream altogether.

We explore dream symbolism, lucid dreaming, recurring dreams, visitation dreams, precognitive dreams, archetypes, subconscious processing, and the possibility that some dreams may involve states of consciousness we do not fully understand.

## Synchronicity, Signs and the Unexplained

Sometimes coincidence is just coincidence.

Sometimes it feels almost impossible to ignore.

Repeated numbers.

A song appearing at the exact moment you need it.

Thinking of someone seconds before they contact you.

A chain of events that seems improbably well timed.

A symbol that keeps appearing.

A message that arrives through several completely unrelated sources.

Carl Jung used the term **synchronicity** to describe meaningful coincidences that appear connected by significance rather than ordinary cause and effect.

We explore those moments — including the possibility that they reveal something about consciousness, attention, meaning, probability, or the structure of reality itself.

## Science at the Edge

Sisters with Mirrors is a spiritual and metaphysical project, but that does not mean science is the enemy of mystery.

Science is one of humanity's best tools for discovering when our assumptions are wrong.

We are interested in research and ideas surrounding consciousness studies, neuroscience, psychology, physics, cosmology, near-death experiences, anomalous cognition, perception, astronomy, the search for extraterrestrial life, and other areas where new discoveries may reshape old assumptions.

We are equally interested in recognising where evidence ends and speculation begins.

Wonder and critical thinking can exist in the same room.

They should.

## We Are Not Here to Give You a Belief System

You do not need to believe in astrology.

You do not need to believe in reincarnation.

You do not need to believe in aliens, mediumship, tarot, spirit communication, higher dimensions, or any particular definition of Source.

You are allowed to be sceptical.

You are allowed to believe.

You are allowed to change your mind.

Sisters with Mirrors is about remaining curious enough to explore extraordinary possibilities without pretending uncertainty does not exist.
