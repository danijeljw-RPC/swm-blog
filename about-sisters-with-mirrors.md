# About Sisters with Mirrors

**Sisters with Mirrors** is a weekly podcast and growing blog exploring the strange, spiritual, cosmic, and deeply human questions that sit just beyond everyday life.

We talk about consciousness, the higher self, Source, reincarnation, the afterlife, psychic and mediumistic experiences, spirituality, energy, astrology, tarot, dreams, synchronicity, extraterrestrial intelligence, higher dimensions, the nature of reality, and everything in between.

Some weeks we follow a single idea. Other weeks we start with one question and end up somewhere completely unexpected.

The point is not to tell you what to believe.

It is to explore, question, compare experiences, look at different perspectives, and stay open to the possibility that reality may be much bigger than the part we can currently see.

Sisters with Mirrors is hosted by **DJ** and **Warren**, two people who approach the unknown from different angles but share the same curiosity about what lies beyond the obvious. The conversations can be serious, philosophical, personal, strange, funny, confronting, and occasionally completely off the rails.

That is part of the show.

Alongside the podcast, the Sisters with Mirrors blog expands the subjects we talk about into articles, explainers, guides, references, and deeper dives. Astrology and tarot are part of that world, but they are only one part of a much larger conversation about consciousness, spirituality, mystery, life, death, the universe, and whatever may exist beyond it.

You do not have to believe in everything we discuss.

You just have to be curious enough to ask the question.

**Open your mind. Question everything. Step through the mirror.**
